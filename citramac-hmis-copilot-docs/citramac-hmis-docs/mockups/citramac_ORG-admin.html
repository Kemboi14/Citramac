Roles & Permissions
Branch-level RBAC — defines what each staff role can see and do, and who is assigned to it.
Psychiatrist
Ward Nurse
Therapist
Clinical Supervisor
Front Desk
Billing Clerk
Psychiatrist — full clinical access: can admit/discharge patients, prescribe, write session notes, and review supervision requests from their team.
Ward Nurse — manages bed status and vitals within an assigned ward; can view but not edit patient registration or billing.
Therapist — runs Individual/Family/Group Psychotherapy sessions and can raise supervision requests; no access to billing or branch settings.
Clinical Supervisor — reviews and closes supervision requests, can view all staff caseloads; read-only on billing.
Front Desk — handles outpatient/CCP registration and appointment check-in; no access to clinical notes or staff management.
Billing Clerk — manages invoices, SHA claims and M-Pesa reconciliation; read-only on ward and staff data.
