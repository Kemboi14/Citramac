Super Admin · Governance
Global Roles & Permissions
Platform-level RBAC — controls what each Softlink staff role can see and do across every tenant.
Super Admin
Support Agent
Billing Admin
Auditor
MFL Verifier
Super Admin — full, unrestricted access to every module across all organizations. Can create, edit, suspend, and delete tenants, branches, roles, and billing records.
Support Agent — can view organizations and branches to assist with tickets, but cannot edit billing or platform-level roles.
Billing Admin — manages subscriptions, plans, and invoices; read-only on organizations and branches.
Auditor — read-only access to the immutable audit log and organization/branch records for DHA compliance reviews.
MFL Verifier — reviews and approves pending organizations against the DHA Master Facility List; cannot touch billing or roles.
